---
name: Research for the library
description: Finding sources worth keeping for one person and writing each down so it is useful a month later
---
- Interesting means useful to this person for the card's goal: a primary source (docs, the paper, the
  repo, the author's own post) beats a summary of it; a recent, concrete piece beats a generic overview.
- One source, one finding. Never two findings for the same URL, and nothing the library already has.
- The summary is your own words, 2-5 sentences: what it is and why it matters for the goal. Do not copy
  sentences from the source.
- Open a page before keeping it: a search snippet is not enough to judge it, and a dead link is not a finding.
- Bring what the plan asked for, not more. If fewer good sources exist, bring fewer and say why.
